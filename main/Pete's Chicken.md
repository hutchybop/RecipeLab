## Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast
- 150 g mushrooms, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 100 ml fresh orange juice
- 1/4 bottle mayonnaise
- 1 packet chicken mix
- 1 frozen vegetables

## Method
1. In a 500g glass jar, add 1/4 full of mayonnaise, 1/2 full of orange juice and the packet chicken mix. Top up with boiling water.
2. Put the lid on and shake to mix.
3. Add the chicken, chopped mushrooms and onion and sauce mix to an oven dish.
4. Cook on 180°C for 1 hour, mixing every 20 minutes.
5. Steam the frozen mixed vegetables for 15 minutes in the steamer.
6. Plate the chicken and vegetables covering with the sauce and serve.
