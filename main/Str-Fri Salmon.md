## Ingredients
- 1 Stir-Fry Sauce
- 1 Stir-Fry Medley, chopped
- 2 Salmon Fillets, diced
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 2 Chilies, minced
- 1 Fresh Ginger, minced
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Noodles, broken up

## Method
1. Mince 2 chilies, 2 garlic cloves, and a thumb of ginger
2. Add the coconut oil, garlic, ginger and chilies to the wok on high heat for 2 minutes
3. Add the salmon and cook until opaque
4. Add the stir-fry medley and cook for 4-5 minutes
5. Add the noodles and the stir-fry sauce and cook for another 3-4 minutes
6. Plate and serve
