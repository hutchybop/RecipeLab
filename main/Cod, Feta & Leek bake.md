## Ingredients
- 2 leeks, chopped
- 2 fish fillets
- 75 g feta cheese, grated
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Chop the leek and mince the garlic.
2. Soften the leek by boiling in water for 5 minutes.
3. Chop the leek, spread in oven dish and add the 2 fish fillets.
4. Cover with olive oil, garlic and broken up feta.
5. Cook for 15-20 minutes (25-30 minutes if frozen).
6. With about 10 minutes to go, microwave 1 handful of frozen vegetables.
7. Serve all together covering with zest of lemon.
