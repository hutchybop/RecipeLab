## Ingredients
- 1 Frozen Broccoli
- 1 Frozen Spinach
- 1 Mixed Beans
- 2 Sweet Potatoes, quartered
- 2 Chilies, minced
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 1 Onion, diced
- 1 UHT Milk
- 1 tsp Paprika
- 1 tsp Mixed Herbs
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil

## Method
1. Cook the sweet potatoes for 30 minutes
2. Mince 2 chilies, 2 garlic cloves and dice the onion
3. After 15 minutes steam the frozen broccoli and spinach in the steamer for 15 minutes
4. After 20-25 minutes, fry the onions, garlic, chilies, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp mixed herbs with 1 tbsp olive oil
5. Add the steamed vegetables to the pan of onions and cook for 2-3 minutes
6. Microwave the beans for 2 minutes 30 seconds
7. Mash the potatoes with some milk
8. Add the vegetables and the mash, top with the beans and serve
