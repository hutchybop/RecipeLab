## Ingredients
- 1 handful frozen pineapple, diced
- 1 handful dried banana chips
- 1 sweet potato, diced
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tin black beans
- 3 limes, juiced
- 1 avocado, chopped
- 3 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- 2 tbsp salsa
- Hot sauce
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Add the diced sweet potato to a baking tray with baking paper on it.
2. Add the diced pepper, onion and frozen pineapple to another baking tray with baking paper on it.
3. Cover both trays each with 1 tsp cumin, 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lime, salt and pepper.
4. Bake the sweet potatoes at 180°C for 30 minutes turning every 10 minutes or so.
5. After 10 minutes add the vegetable tray and bake for 20 minutes turning half way through.
6. Also after 10 minutes add the black beans, 1 tsp cumin, juice of 1 lime and a little boiling water to a pan and bring to the boil. Then simmer for 10-15 minutes.
7. While everything is cooking add the avocado, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp cumin, a little salt and a dash of water to a blender. Blend until smooth. Add more water if required.
8. Add everything plus the dried banana chips, salsa, hot sauce and pumpkin seeds to a large bowl and serve.
