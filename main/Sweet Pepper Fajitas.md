## Ingredients
- 2 Onions, diced
- 4 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 6 Bell Peppers, cut into strips
- 6 Chilies, minced
- 2/3 cup Brown Rice
- 2 Mixed Beans
- 2 Salsa
- 2 Tortillas
- 1 tbsp Dark Brown Sugar
- 2 tsp Chili Powder
- 2 tsp Cumin
- 2 tsp Paprika
- 2 tsp Cayenne Pepper
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- Sea Salt
- Black Pepper
- 2 tsp Turmeric
- 6 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil

## Method
1. Put 2/3 cup rice, 4 cups water and 2 tsp turmeric. Bring to boil and simmer till water absorbed
2. Large frying pan on a medium heat with 4 tbsp olive oil
3. Chop onion and mince 2-3 chilies and 2 garlic cloves
4. Cut 3 peppers into strips
5. Add garlic and chilies to pan and heat for 2 minutes
6. Add onion and peppers heat for 1 minute
7. Sprinkle over 1 tbsp brown sugar and salt
8. Mix 2 tsp hot chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp cayenne pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt and pepper and stir and add to the peppers
9. Reduce the heat a little, then cook for 30 minutes until soft
10. Cook the beans with 5 minutes to go
11. Make fajitas with salsa and hot sauce
