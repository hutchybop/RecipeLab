## Ingredients
- 2 kg Ham
- 1 Carrot, thickly sliced
- 1 Onion, quartered
- 2 L Cider
- 1 Croquettes, 1/2 bag
- 2 Eggs
- 500 ml Apple Juice
- 2 tsp Mixed Herbs
- 3 tbsp Honey
- 3 tbsp Dark Brown Sugar
- 1 tsp Dijon Mustard

## Method
1. Quarter the onion and thickly slice the carrot
2. Rub the mixed herbs into the ham
3. Put the ham, onion, carrot and cider into the slow cooker. Top up with apple juice
4. Cook for 6-8 hours on low
5. Once cooked, remove the ham. Remove the string and layer of fat/skin
6. Score the remaining fat into crosses
7. Put the ham into a roasting pan lined with tin foil. Drench in honey and pat the sugar onto the honey
8. Cook in the oven with the croquettes at 200°C for 30 minutes
9. Blend the leftover sauce to make gravy
10. With 5 minutes left, fry 2 eggs
11. Serve the ham, sliced, with the croquettes, eggs and gravy
