## Ingredients
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained
- 1 onion, quartered
- 2 chilies, minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 pita breads
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tsp parsley
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1/2 bag croquettes

## Method
1. Add all the ingredients apart from the croquettes and tomatoes to the food processor.
2. Process until fully mixed, stop before you get a hummus consistency.
3. Make 6-9 small patties with hands.
4. Place the patties on a baking tray covered with baking paper and sprinkle over a little olive oil.
5. Bake for 40 minutes on 180°C, turning every 5-10 minutes.
6. After 10-15 minutes add the croquettes to the oven.
7. Serve with the sliced tomatoes and pita breads.
