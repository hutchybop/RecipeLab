## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 shallots, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 8-10 sun-dried tomatoes
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp basil
- 200 ml chicken stock
- 1 tin butter beans
- 3 tbsp crème fraîche
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 30-50 g parmesan cheese
- 3 blocks frozen spinach
- Pinch of sea salt
- Pinch of black pepper

## Method
1. Season the salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Heat a frying pan with a splash of oil until hot, then add the salmon and sear on all sides until coloured. Remove and set to one side.
2. In the same pan, reduce the heat a little, add 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil, then sauté the diced shallot and minced garlic with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
3. Add the halved cherry tomatoes, 8-10 sun-dried tomatoes, 1 tsp paprika and basil. Sauté for a few minutes.
4. Add the 200ml chicken stock, butter beans and 3 frozen spinach blocks. Simmer until the spinach has defrosted and cooked (around 5-10 minutes).
5. Reduce the heat to low, then mix in the 3 tbsp crème fraîche, lemon zest, 30-50g parmesan, 1 tsp basil and a crack of black pepper.
6. Position the salmon fillets in the pan, cover with the sauce and allow them to warm and cook through for around 5 minutes.
7. Serve sprinkled with extra basil and grating of parmesan.
