## Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast, chopped
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 sugar snap peas, chopped
- 2 sweet potatoes, quartered
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- UHT milk

## Method
1. Boil chopped potatoes for 25-30 minutes.
2. Chop garlic and slice the chicken into thin strips.
3. Add the chicken, garlic, salt, pepper and 1 tbsp olive oil to wok and leave to marinate.
4. With 5-10 minutes left on the potatoes, cook the chicken then transfer to a plate.
5. Add a little more olive oil to the wok and fry the peas for 5 minutes.
6. Mash the potatoes with some milk and add the peas and mash to the plate.
7. Pour over the lemon juice and serve.
