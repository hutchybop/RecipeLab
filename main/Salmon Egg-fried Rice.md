## Ingredients
- 1 stir-fry medley
- 1/2 cup brown rice
- 2 eggs
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 fresh ginger, minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 chilies, minced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 salmon fillets

## Method
1. Cook 1/2 cup of brown rice in a pan of boiling water for 25 minutes.
2. Mince 2 chilies, 2 garlic cloves and thumb sized ginger.
3. Chop the stir-fry medley.
4. Mix the chilies, garlic, ginger, 2 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp rice wine.
5. With 5-10 minutes left on the rice, fry the salmon in the wok with a little olive oil for 5 minutes.
6. Add the stir-fry medley and fry for 2-3 minutes.
7. Add the cooked rice and the mixed sauce and stir in for 2 minutes.
8. Crack 2 eggs in and mix until cooked.
9. Serve.
