## Ingredients
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 chopped tomatoes
- 2 fish fillets
- 1 frozen broccoli
- 1 frozen spinach
- 2 tsp basil
- 1 tsp coriander
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic.
2. Sauté the onion and garlic in a frying pan with oil for 5-10 minutes.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes and bring to gentle simmer.
4. Add the fish and cover with the lid. Poach for 10-20 minutes.
5. With 10 minutes to go, steam the broccoli in the microwave for 5-6 minutes.
6. Add the broccoli to the blender with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp basil, salt and pepper and blend until a chunky mash.
7. Serve together with a sprinkle of basil.
