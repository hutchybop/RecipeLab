# Tasting Profile

## Core Taste Preferences (Global)

The user demonstrates a strong preference for **savory, spice-forward dishes** with balanced complexity. Recipes consistently feature moderate to high spice levels (chili, paprika, cumin, curry powder) with strong emphasis on **herbaceous and aromatic flavors** (coriander, basil, garlic, ginger). Sweet flavors are primarily confined to dessert contexts, with main dishes rarely incorporating sweet elements beyond natural ingredient sweetness.

There is a clear preference for **umami-rich preparations** through stock bases, fermented ingredients (miso paste), and tomatoes. The user favors dishes with **textural contrast** rather than uniform textures, combining creamy elements with crunch or firm components.

## Ingredient & Protein Preferences

**Strong Protein Signals:**
- **Legumes dominate** as preferred protein source: lentils (red, green), black beans, mixed beans, cannellini beans, butter beans appear across 15+ recipes
- **Chicken** is the primary animal protein, featured in 5+ recipes with consistent 6-7 ratings
- **Salmon** shows strong preference (3 recipes, all rated 6-7), preferred over other fish types
- **Eggs** appear in multiple contexts, indicating flexibility and acceptance

**Moderate Protein Signals:**
- Beef appears in 2 recipes (brisket, steak) but with limited frequency
- Plant-based proteins clearly preferred over meat in overall quantity

**Preferred Ingredients:**
- **Alliums**: Heavy use of onions, garlic, shallots across nearly all recipes
- **Tomatoes**: Both fresh (cherry, chopped) and processed (passata, tinned) appear frequently
- **Leafy greens**: Spinach (6+ appearances), kale, pak choy indicate preference for nutrient-dense vegetables
- **Bell peppers**: Consistent presence across multiple cuisines and dishes
- **Healthy fats**: Extra virgin olive oil is the universal cooking fat, nuts (cashews, almonds) for texture and nutrition
- **Fermented foods**: Miso paste, kimchi, apple cider vinegar show emerging preference for umami complexity

## Flavour & Cuisine Affinities

**Strong Cuisine Affinities:**
- **Indian-inspired**: Dhal varieties, curry powder, turmeric, cumin seeds, garam masala appear in 6+ recipes
- **Asian-inspired**: Soy sauce, ginger, miso, rice wine vinegar, kimchi in 5+ recipes (emerging stronger pattern)
- **Mediterranean**: Olive oil, lemon, herbs, tomatoes feature prominently in 4+ recipes

**Flavour Profile Preferences:**
- **Spice tolerance**: Moderate-high, with fresh chilies and chili flakes regularly used
- **Herb-forward**: Coriander, basil, oregano used frequently and generously
- **Acid balance**: Lemon juice and vinegar used as finishing touches for brightness
- **Aromatic base**: Garlic-ginger-chili trio forms foundation of many dishes

## Texture & Dish-Type Preferences

**Preferred Textures:**
- **Hearty, substantial**: Stews, curries, thick soups
- **Varied mouthfeel**: Combination of creamy (cashew cream, coconut milk) with firm (beans, vegetables) and crunchy (nuts, seeds)
- **Avoids mushy preparations**: Even legume-based dishes maintain distinct component textures

**Dish Type Preferences:**
- **One-pot/one-pan meals**: Strong preference for minimal cleanup (8+ recipes)
- **Stew and curry formats**: Dominant main meal preparation style
- **Salads with substantial components**: Quinoa salads with protein and varied vegetables
- **Soups**: Bean and tomato-based, preferably hearty rather than brothy

## Cooking Behaviour & Constraints

**Time Preferences:**
- **Weekday meals**: 25-40 minute total time preferred (prep + cook)
- **Weekend meals**: Willing to spend 45-75 minutes for more complex dishes
- **Batch cooking**: Strong preference for weekday lunches (6+ recipes explicitly marked for batch cooking)

**Difficulty Threshold:**
- **Comfortable range**: Difficulty 4-6 out of 10
- **Avoids**: Highly technical preparations (difficulty 7+ acceptable only for special occasions)
- **Preferred techniques**: Sautéing, simmering, baking; minimal deep frying or complex multi-stage processes

**Serving Patterns:**
- **Weekday lunches**: Primarily batch-cooked, vegetarian, single-serving reheated
- **Main meals**: 1-3 servings, with freezer-friendly options valued
- **Portion awareness**: Most recipes scaled for 1-3 people, indicating household size

## Dislikes, Avoidance Signals & Red Flags

**Explicit Dislikes (Hard Constraints):**

- **Peanuts** (including peanut butter and peanut sauces)
- **Sprouts** (all types)
- **Cauliflower** (any preparation)
- **Cinnamon** (any preparation)

**Inferred Avoidance Signals:**
- **Dairy-heavy preparations**: Limited cheese usage, primarily parmesan as seasoning
- **Deep-fried foods**: No deep frying techniques in any recipes
- **Pasta dishes**: Limited Italian cuisine beyond tomato-based preparations
- **Highly processed ingredients**: Preference for whole foods over convenience products
- **Baked goods**: Sweet preferences limited to specific dessert contexts

## Ideal Constraints for New Recipe Suggestions

**Must-Have Criteria:**
- Contains at least one preferred protein: beans, lentils, chicken, or salmon
- Features aromatic base: garlic, onion, or shallots
- Includes spice element: chili, cumin, paprika, curry powder, or miso
- Total time under 45 minutes for weekday suggestions

**Preferred but Flexible:**
- One-pan or one-pot preparation method
- Serves 1-4 people with freezing potential
- Incorporates healthy fats: olive oil, nuts, or avocado
- Includes fresh herbs or citrus for brightness

**Should Avoid:**
- Whole chickpeas, peanuts, sprouts, cauliflower, or cinnamon in any form
- Deep frying techniques
- Heavy dairy reliance
- Pasta as main component
- Highly processed convenience foods
