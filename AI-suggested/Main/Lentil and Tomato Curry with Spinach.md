## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 1 green chili, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 200 g red lentils, rinsed
- 400 ml vegetable stock
- 200 ml coconut milk
- 3 large handfuls of spinach
- 1 lime, juiced
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt to taste

## Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add cumin seeds and toast for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add onion, garlic, ginger, and green chili. Sauté for 5-6 minutes until onion is softened.
4. Add turmeric, garam masala, and curry powder. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add chopped tomatoes and cook for 3-4 minutes until they start to break down.
6. Stir in red lentils, vegetable stock, and coconut milk. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer.
7. Cover and cook for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are tender and curry has thickened.
8. Remove from heat and stir in spinach until wilted.
9. Add lime juice and fresh coriander. Season with salt to taste.
10. Serve hot with brown rice, quinoa, or naan bread.

## Notes
Excellent source of plant-based protein and fiber. Freezes well for up to 3 months. Adjust spice levels by adding or reducing green chili.
