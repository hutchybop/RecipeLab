## Ingredients
- 2 tins cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup pearl barley, cooked
- 1 cup farro, cooked
- 2 bell peppers, diced (1 red, 1 yellow)
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved
- 200 g feta cheese, crumbled
- 3 tbsp capers, drained
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint, chopped
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 5 tbsp toasted pine nuts

## Method
1. Cook pearl barley and farro separately according to package instructions. Let cool completely.
2. In a large bowl, combine cooled grains with cannellini beans, diced bell peppers, red onion, cherry tomatoes, olives, and capers.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, red wine vinegar, minced garlic, dried oregano, and red pepper flakes to create the dressing.
4. Pour dressing over the grain and bean mixture. Toss gently to combine.
5. Add crumbled feta cheese and fresh herbs (basil, parsley, mint). Toss again gently.
6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
7. Divide into 5 airtight containers.
8. Top each portion with 1 tbsp toasted pine nuts just before serving.
9. Can be served cold or at room temperature - no heating required.

## Notes
Hearty Mediterranean grain bowl with complete protein from beans and grains combination. The flavors meld beautifully overnight, making it ideal for meal prep. Feta adds creaminess while pine nuts provide crunch. Can substitute barley with quinoa for gluten-free option. Lasts 5 days in refrigerator.
