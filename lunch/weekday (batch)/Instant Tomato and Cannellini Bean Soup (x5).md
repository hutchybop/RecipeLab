## Ingredients
- 4 tins chopped tomatoes
- 2 tins cannellini beans
- ½ spring onions, chopped
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 tsp basil
- 2 ½ pita bread

## Method
1. Add 4 tins of chopped tomatoes, 2 tins of cannellini beans, spring onion loosely chopped, 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, sea salt, black pepper, 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar and basil to a food processor.
2. Mix until smooth.
3. Divide into 5 portions.
4. Heat 1 portion for 2 minutes 30 seconds in microwave and serve with a pita bread.
