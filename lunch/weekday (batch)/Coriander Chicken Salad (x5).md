## Ingredients
- 2 avocados, diced
- 300 g chicken breast, diced
- 2 tsp coriander
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 limes, juiced
- 2 onions, diced
- Black pepper
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- Nandos sauce
- Mayonnaise
- 2 bell peppers, chopped

## Method
1. Mince the garlic.
2. Dice the chicken.
3. Add the chicken, a little olive oil, half of the garlic, 1-2 tsp coriander, juice of 1 lime, salt and black pepper to the pan to marinate.
4. Halve the tomatoes, dice the avocados and onions.
5. Start to cook the chicken.
6. Add 1-2 tsp of coriander, juice of 1 lime, tomatoes, onion, avocados, 1-2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, sea salt and black pepper to a bowl and mix.
7. Once the chicken is cooked and cooled, divide the salad and chicken into 5 portions.
