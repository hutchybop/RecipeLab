## Ingredients
- 2 bell peppers, halved
- 1 tin mixed beans
- 1/2 cup brown rice
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 chilies, minced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 tsp turmeric

## Method
1. Put 1/2 cup rice, 2 cups water and 1 tsp turmeric. Bring to boil and simmer till water absorbed (about 30 minutes).
2. Cut pepper in half and put on baking paper in oven on 180°C for 30 minutes.
3. Chop garlic and chilies and add to cooked rice.
4. Cook beans.
5. Add rice and beans on top of peppers.
6. Serve.
