## Ingredients
- 1 tin mixed beans
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin black beans, drained
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 bell peppers, diced
- 1/3 cup couscous
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp coriander
- 2 tsp paprika
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 3 chillies, minced

## Method
1. Chop the peppers and onions.
2. Mince 4 garlic cloves and 4 chillies.
3. Fry the garlic and chillies for 1-2 minutes with 1 tbsp olive oil.
4. Add the peppers and onion for another 5-10 minutes.
5. Add 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp coriander, 2 tsp paprika, salt and black pepper and mix in for 1-2 minutes.
6. Add tin of mixed beans, tin of chopped tomatoes and tin of black beans and bring to boil.
7. Simmer for 30 minutes.
8. Divide to 5 portions.
9. Heat 1 portion in the microwave for 2 minutes 30 seconds.
10. To serve, cook boil 1.5 1/3 cups of boiling water and 1/3 cup couscous with 1 tsp turmeric.

## Notes
Makes 5 servings for batch lunches
