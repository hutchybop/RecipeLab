## Ingredients
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 celery sticks, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1 courgette, chopped
- 1 tin cannellini beans, drained
- 1 tin plum tomatoes
- 3 vegetable stock cubes, 800ml
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 50 g kale
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 5 pita bread

## Method
1. Chop the onion, 1 carrot, 1 garlic clove and celery.
2. Fry the veg with 1 tbsp olive oil for 10 minutes.
3. Make 800ml stock then chop 1 carrot and a courgette.
4. Add the stock, carrot, courgette, tin of plum tomatoes, tin of cannellini beans, salt and black pepper.
5. Bring to boil then simmer for 30 minutes.
6. Chop and add the kale for another 5 minutes.
7. Process the soup in a food blender until smooth.
8. Divide into 5 portions.
9. Cook 1 portion for 2 minutes 30 seconds in the microwave and serve with pita bread.
