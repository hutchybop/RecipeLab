## Ingredients
- Rice cakes
- 650 g chicken breast
- 5 small natural yoghurt
- 5 lemons, zest
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- Rocket
- 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Cook batch chicken and store in 100g bags (oven bake).
2. Mix 100g chicken, 75ml yogurt, lemon zest, chilli flakes, salt and pepper.
3. Top rice cakes with rocket.
4. Add chicken mix.
5. Sprinkle over 1 tsp pumpkin seeds.
6. Serve.
