## Ingredients
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 3 bell peppers, diced
- 5 lemons, juiced
- 5 tbsp almonds, ground
- 1/3 cup and 1/2 cup quinoa
- 300 g chicken breast
- 5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 5 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 5 tsp chilli flakes
- Nandos sauce
- Mayonnaise

## Method
1. Cook the chicken in the oven for 30 minutes.
2. Cook 1 tsp turmeric with 1/2 and 1/3 cup quinoa with x3 1/2 cup and x3 1/3 cup of water for 15 minutes.
3. Dice 3 peppers and 1 cucumber.
4. Grind 5 tbsp almonds.
5. Dice the chicken and allow both the chicken and quinoa to cool.
6. Divide chicken, quinoa, cucumber, rocket and pepper into 5 portions.
7. Divide into 5 portions the pepper, cucumber, quinoa, rocket, chicken.
8. Add 1 tbsp almonds, 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds, salt, black pepper and chilli flakes to each portion.
9. Serve each portion with the juice of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil and a dash of balsamic vinegar.
