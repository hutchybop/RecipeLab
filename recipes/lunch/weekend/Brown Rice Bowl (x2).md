## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup brown rice
- 1/2 pack mushrooms, chopped
- 3 blocks frozen spinach
- 1 egg
- 1/2 avocado, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- 1 tsp turmeric
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Cook 1/3 cup of rice in pan of boiling water for 30 minutes with a sprinkle of turmeric.
2. With 10-15 minutes left on the rice, cook the mushrooms with 1 tbsp olive oil until golden and reduced slightly (10 minutes).
3. While the mushrooms are cooking, poach the egg for about 6-7 minutes.
4. Plate up the mushrooms.
5. In the same pan, cook the spinach with the garlic until slightly wilted (2-3 minutes).
6. Add the rice, avocado, egg and spinach to the plate and serve.
