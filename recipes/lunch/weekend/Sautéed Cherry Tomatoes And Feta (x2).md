## Ingredients
- 1 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 50 g feta cheese, grated
- 1 sourdough
- 15 g pine nuts
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Sea salt
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Halve the cherry tomatoes.
2. Heat the cherry tomatoes in a sauté pan, drizzle with 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, add 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 1 tsp basil, salt and pepper.
3. After 3-4 minutes add the pine nuts.
4. After another 3-4 minutes toast the sourdough bread.
5. Serve the tomatoes and pine nuts on the bread and grate the feta cheese over the top.
