## Ingredients
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 chili, minced
- 1/2 tin black beans, drained
- 1 egg
- 1 pita bread
- 1/2 avocado, chopped
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Sea salt
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp coriander
- 90 g cashews
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Add 90g cashews, lemon juice, 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar and 3-4 tbsp of water and blend to double cream consistency, add more water if required.
2. Chop 1/2 onion, 1 garlic clove and 1/2 avocado.
3. Poach the egg for 6 minutes 30 seconds.
4. Add olive oil, garlic, chili and onion to pan fry for 3-4 minutes.
5. Toast pita bread.
6. Add drained 1/2 can of black beans and 1 tsp paprika to pan.
7. Serve as follows: pita bread, onion and black beans, avocado, cashew cream, dried coriander and chili flakes, poached egg.
