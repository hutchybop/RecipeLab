## Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast, diced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tin black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 200 ml chicken stock
- 2 cups spinach leaves
- 1 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat.
2. Add diced chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove from pan and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add onion, bell pepper, garlic, and ginger. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until vegetables start to soften.
4. Add chili flakes, paprika, and cumin. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add black beans, soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, and chicken stock. Bring to a simmer and cook for 3-4 minutes.
6. Return chicken to the pan along with spinach leaves. Stir until spinach wilts (about 1-2 minutes).
7. Remove from heat and stir in fresh coriander.
8. Serve hot with lime wedges on the side.

## Notes
Serve with brown rice or quinoa for a complete meal. Freezer-friendly for up to 3 months.
