## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 courgette, cut into chunks
- 200 g green beans, trimmed
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2 spring onions, sliced

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C/180°C fan (400°F) Gas Mark 6.
2. In a small bowl, mix miso paste, rice wine vinegar, honey, and sesame oil until smooth.
3. Pat salmon fillets dry and brush with half of the miso mixture.
4. On a large baking tray, toss bell pepper, red onion, courgette, green beans, garlic, and ginger with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
5. Spread vegetables in a single layer and roast for 15 minutes.
6. Push vegetables to one side of the tray and place salmon fillets on the other side.
7. Drizzle remaining miso mixture over salmon and return to oven for 12-15 minutes until salmon is cooked through.
8. Drizzle soy sauce over vegetables and salmon, sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions.
9. Serve hot with rice or as is for a low-carb option.

## Notes
High in protein and omega-3 fatty acids. The miso glaze can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to a week.
