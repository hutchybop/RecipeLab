## Ingredients
- 200 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tin full-fat coconut milk
- 400 ml vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp red curry paste
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 red chillies, thinly sliced
- 2 bell peppers, cut into strips
- 200 g green beans, trimmed and cut in half
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 3 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add onions and sauté for 5-6 minutes until softened.
3. Add garlic, ginger, and red chillies. Cook for 2 minutes until fragrant.
4. Add red curry paste and turmeric, stir for 1 minute until aromatic.
5. Add chopped tomatoes (if using) and cook for 2 minutes.
6. Stir in red lentils, coconut milk, and vegetable stock. Bring to a gentle boil.
7. Reduce heat to simmer, cover and cook for 15 minutes.
8. Add bell peppers and green beans, cook for another 8-10 minutes until lentils are tender and vegetables are cooked.
9. Stir in soy sauce and lime juice. Add fresh coriander.
10. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
11. Divide into 5 portions in microwave-safe containers.
12. Reheat in microwave for 2-3 minutes before serving.

## Notes
Creamy, protein-rich curry that thickens nicely when reheated. The flavors develop overnight, making it even more delicious the next day. Can be served over rice or as is for a lighter option. Lasts 5 days refrigerated.
