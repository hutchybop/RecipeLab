## Ingredients
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 2 tins black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tin red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 1 red onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp cumin powder
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 3 red chillies, minced
- 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 limes, juiced
- 3 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 5 tbsp pumpkin seeds for garnish

## Method
1. Rinse the quinoa under cold water until the water runs clear.
2. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
3. Add diced onion and bell peppers, sauté for 5-6 minutes until softened.
4. Add minced garlic and red chillies, cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
5. Add cumin, smoked paprika, and coriander powder, stir for 30 seconds until aromatic.
6. Add chopped tomatoes, black beans, and kidney beans. Mix well.
7. Add quinoa and 2.5 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to simmer.
8. Cover and cook for 15-18 minutes until quinoa is cooked and liquid is absorbed.
9. Remove from heat and let stand for 5 minutes.
10. Stir in lime juice and fresh coriander. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
11. Divide into 5 portions for lunch. Store in airtight containers.
12. To serve, reheat in microwave for 2-3 minutes and top with pumpkin seeds.

## Notes
High-protein vegetarian lunch with complete amino acids from quinoa and beans combination. Reheats well in microwave and maintains texture. Can be served cold as a salad in warmer weather. Lasts 5 days in refrigerator.
