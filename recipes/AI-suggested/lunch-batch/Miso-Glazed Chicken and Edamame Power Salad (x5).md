## Ingredients
- 400 g chicken breast, diced
- 200 g shelled edamame, cooked
- 2 cups mixed greens (spinach and rocket)
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- 2 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 red chillies, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- 5 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Toss diced chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 20-25 minutes until cooked through. Let cool completely.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together miso paste, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, minced garlic, and grated ginger to create the dressing.
3. Cook edamame according to package instructions, then rinse under cold water and drain well.
4. In a large bowl, combine mixed greens, diced cucumber, julienned carrots, and sliced red onion.
5. Add cooled chicken and edamame to the vegetables.
6. Pour the miso dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine.
7. Divide into 5 airtight containers.
8. Top each portion with fresh coriander, sliced red chillies, and 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds.
9. Store in refrigerator - can be eaten cold directly from container.

## Notes
Protein-rich salad that maintains texture when served cold. The miso dressing provides umami depth without requiring heating. Chicken can be grilled instead of roasted for variation. Edamame adds complete protein alongside chicken. Lasts 4 days in refrigerator.
