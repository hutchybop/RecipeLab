## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 Leek, chopped
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup Pearl Barley
- 70 g Puy Lentils
- 1/2 cup White Wine
- 400 g Asparagus, chopped
- 50 g Parmesan cheese, grated
- 3 tbsp Kefir
- 2 tsp Parsley
- Sea Salt
- Black Pepper
- 400 ml Vegetable Stock

## Method
1. Add 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil and leek to a sauté pan and fry on medium heat for 5 minutes
2. Add the garlic and fry for another 2-3 minutes
3. Add 1/2 cup barley, lentils and 1/2 cup wine and simmer for 1 minute
4. Then add 400ml broth and bring to the boil then simmer with the lid on for 15 minutes
5. Add the grated parmesan and asparagus and simmer for another 15 minutes
6. Remove the heat and let cool for 2-3 minutes. If there is too much water simmer with lid off for 5-10 minutes
7. Add 3 tbsp kefir, 2 tsp parsley, salt and pepper
8. Split in two, freeze one and serve the other
