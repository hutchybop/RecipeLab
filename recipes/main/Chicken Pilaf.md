## Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast
- 1 onion, diced
- 80 g sultanas
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 40 g pine nuts
- Fresh mint leaves, chopped
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 200 g brown rice
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 750 ml vegetable stock

## Method
1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Seal the chicken until brown with 1 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil, then remove the chicken and cut into strips.
3. Add onions and 1 tsp cumin to the same pan and fry.
4. Add rice, sultanas, 2 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp black pepper and lemon zest and cook together for about a minute.
5. Add 750ml chicken stock and the chicken strips, bring to a simmer. Cover with a tightly fitting lid, reduce to low heat and cook for 40 to 50 minutes.
6. Once cooked, set the pilaf aside, covered.
7. Toast the pine nuts. As soon as they are slightly golden, add a teaspoon of olive oil and a pinch of salt and mix well to coat.
8. Add pine nuts and chopped mint leaves.
9. Serve.

## Notes
There should be enough for leftovers.
