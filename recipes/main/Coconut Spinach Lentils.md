## Ingredients
- 300 g frozen spinach
- 130 g cashew nuts
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 1 tin green lentils
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 chili, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 vegetable stock cube
- Sea salt
- Black pepper

## Method
1. Add 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil and onion and sauté for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and chilis for another minute.
3. Stir in 1 tbsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chili powder for another minute.
4. Add the coconut milk and the lentils with the water.
5. Mix a vegetable stock cube with just under half a tin of water and add.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Add spinach and cashews and simmer for another 10 minutes.
8. Split in two if freezing half and serve.
