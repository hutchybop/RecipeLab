## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Brown Rice
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- 1 can Broad Beans in water, drained
- 1 Romano Pepper, chopped
- 150 g Kale, stems removed and chopped
- 1 Pak Choy, chopped
- 50 g Kimchi
- 1 Egg
- 3 tbsp Tomato Puree
- 1 tbsp Honey
- 1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper
- 1 tbsp Red Miso Paste
- Black Pepper
- 1 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
- 2 Chilies, minced
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced

## Method
1. Cook brown rice for 15-20 minutes
2. While rice is cooking, prep the kale, peppers and pak choy
3. Mix all the ingredients for the sauce
4. With 6-7 minutes left on rice, start to fry the egg in the olive oil
5. While the egg and rice are cooking, fry the drained broad beans in the wok with 1 tsp coconut oil for 2 minutes
6. Add the kale, pepper and pak choy and fry for another 2 minutes
7. When rice is done add a little of the sauce
8. Add the rest of the sauce to the vegetables
9. Plate the rice and vegetables with the kimchi and add the egg. Serve
