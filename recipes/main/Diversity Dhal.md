## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 fresh ginger, minced
- 1 chili, minced
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 cup red lentils
- 1/4 cup yellow split peas
- 60 g frozen spinach
- 1/2 butternut squash, diced
- 700 ml vegetable stock

## Method
1. Sauté the onion with 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add the ginger, garlic, chili, 1 tbsp curry powder, 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 tsp garam masala. Cook for another minute.
3. Add 1/4 cup lentils, 1/4 cup yellow split peas and 700ml stock and bring to the boil.
4. Simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Add the butternut squash and simmer for a further 30 minutes.
6. With 10 minutes left add the spinach.
7. Season with salt and pepper and serve.
