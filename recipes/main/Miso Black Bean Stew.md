## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 leek, chopped
- 1 asparagus, chopped
- 150 g kale, stems removed, chopped
- 1 tin black beans, drained
- 2 tsp red miso paste
- 1/2 cup brown rice
- 1 tsp turmeric

## Method
1. Cook the brown rice with water and 1 tsp turmeric for 30 minutes.
2. With about 10 minutes left on the rice, sauté the leek, asparagus and kale in 2 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil for about 3 minutes.
3. Stir in the black beans for another 5 minutes.
4. Mix 2 tbsp miso paste with 2 tbsp coconut oil and 100ml hot (not boiling) water.
5. Add the sauce to the vegetables and beans.
6. Serve with brown rice.
