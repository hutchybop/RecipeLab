## Ingredients
- 1/2 bag oven chips
- 4 burgers
- Cheese, grated
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 bread buns

## Method
1. Cook the oven chips on 180°C for about 30 minutes.
2. After 10 minutes, grill the 4 burgers on each side for 5-10 minutes.
3. Chop the onion and tomatoes.
4. Grate the cheese.
5. Place 2 burgers, the onion, tomatoes and cheese on each bun.
6. Serve.
