## Ingredients
- 1 Onion, diced
- 1 Carrot, chopped
- 4 Celery Sticks, chopped
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 2 Chilies, minced
- 300 g Chicken Breast, chopped
- 1 Bell Pepper, chopped
- 1 Passata
- 1 Cherry Tomatoes, halved
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- 2 tsp Paprika
- 2 tsp Curry Powder
- 2 tsp Chili Powder
- 2 tsp Dried Oregano
- 2 tsp Cumin
- 1 tbsp Tomato Puree
- 1/2 cup Quinoa

## Method
1. Chop onion, celery sticks, carrot, pepper, and mince 2 garlic cloves and 2 chilies
2. Halve the cherry tomatoes
3. Add olive oil, garlic and chilies and sauté for 2 minutes
4. Add Chicken and seal
5. Add 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder and 1 tsp dried oregano and cook for another 2-4 minutes
6. Add onion, celery and carrot to wok stir for 2-4 minutes
7. Add 1 tsp curry powder and cook for another 2 minutes
8. Add cherry tomatoes, 1 tbsp tomato purée and passata
9. Bring to boil and simmer for 30 minutes
10. After 15 minutes, add 1 tsp turmeric, 1/2 cup quinoa with 1 1/2 cups of boiling water to a pan and simmer for 15 minutes
11. When cooked, save 2 portions of curry for freezing
12. Serve 1 portion of curry and the quinoa

## Notes
Makes 3 portions, freeze 2.
