## Ingredients
- 500 g beef mince
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 150 g mushrooms, chopped
- 1 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 passata
- 1/2 cup quinoa
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 chilies, minced
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 blocks frozen spinach
- 1 tbsp tomato purée

## Method
1. Mince 4 chilies and 3 garlic cloves.
2. Chop the pepper, onion, mushrooms, and halve the cherry tomatoes.
3. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil with the minced chilies and garlic.
4. Add the mince and cook until browned (5-10 minutes).
5. Add 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cayenne pepper, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp basil, 1 tsp oregano, salt and pepper.
6. Add 1 handful of spinach bit by bit until wilted.
7. Add the pepper, onion, mushrooms and mix in for 4-5 minutes.
8. Add the tomatoes and tomato purée then mix in for 3-4 minutes.
9. Add passata and simmer for 30 minutes.
10. After 15 minutes, add 1 tsp turmeric, 1/2 cup quinoa with 1 and 1/2 cups of boiling water to a pan and simmer for 15 minutes.
11. When cooked, save 2 portions of SpagBog for freezing.
12. Serve 1 portion of SpagBog and the quinoa.
