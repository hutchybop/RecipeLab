## Ingredients
- 1 carrot, quartered
- 1/2 cup red lentils
- 1/3 cup brown rice
- 600 ml vegetable stock
- 3 jalapeño peppers
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 fresh ginger, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- Sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 pita bread
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Boil kettle and make 600ml of stock with 2 stock cubes.
2. Add carrot, jalapeños, chopped garlic and grated ginger to food processor and run the chop program 2-3 times (or just finely chop everything).
3. Fry the chopped ingredients with the coconut oil for 2-3 mins.
4. Stir in the cumin and turmeric for another 2 mins.
5. Add 1/2 cup red lentils and 1/4 cup brown rice and stir for 2 mins.
6. Add the stock and bring to boil.
7. Reduce to a low heat, add the pan lid slightly ajar and simmer for 1 hr.
8. Once the consistency isn't too watery remove the heat and stir in the coriander and lime juice.
9. Serve with the pita bread.
