## Ingredients
- 1 Asparagus, chopped
- 2 Garlic Cloves, minced
- 1 Fresh Ginger, minced
- 2 Chilies, minced
- 1 Spring Onions, chopped
- 1 Bell Peppers, diced
- 2 Thin Steaks, chopped
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch
- 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
- 1 tbsp Rice Wine Vinegar
- 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil

## Method
1. Mince 2 chilies, 2 garlic cloves and thumb of ginger
2. Mix the chilies, garlic, ginger, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar and 1 tbsp cornstarch
3. Cut the thin steaks into thin slices and fry with olive oil until brown (3-4 minutes)
4. Remove the steak and place on a plate
5. Chop the asparagus, spring onions and pepper and fry with a little more olive oil for 5 minutes
6. Re-add the steak and the sauce mix and fry for another 2-3 minutes
7. Add to plate and serve
