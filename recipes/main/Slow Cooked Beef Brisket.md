## Ingredients
- 1.2 kg beef brisket
- 1 garlic bulb, peeled
- 1 onion, quartered
- 1/2 bottle red wine
- 1 beef stock cube, crumbled
- 3 tbsp tomato purée
- 3 tsp woody herbs
- Black pepper
- 1 gravy mix
- 2 sweet potatoes, quartered
- UHT milk
- 1 handful frozen vegetables

## Method
1. Add all ingredients apart from the sweet potato, milk and frozen mixed veg to the slow cooker.
2. Top the slow cooker up with boiling water to cover the brisket.
3. Slow cook for 8-10 hours.
4. Just before ready, cook the sweet potato for 30 minutes.
5. After 15 minutes steam the mixed frozen veg for 15 minutes.
6. Mash the potatoes with milk.
7. Add all the leftover brisket sauce and veg to a food processor and process. Add gravy mix to thicken.
8. Split the brisket and gravy 4 ways, freeze 3 and plate 1.
9. Plate the veg, mash, brisket and gravy and serve.

## Notes
Will freeze.
